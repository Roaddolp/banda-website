Bassist

Bam

Says nothing. Means every note.

Read Bam's profile

Bam is a chill and quiet guy, BANDA!'s bassist. He prioritizes comfort and functionality over aesthetics, so he wears a cozy, laid-back outfit. He is the calmest member of the group, enjoys taking things slow and steady, and wears a yellow bandana that represents his assigned color.

Logo Design
and Development.

✳

01 /

Initial Sketches
& Design

Different lettering styles, arrangements, and compositions were sketched to explore how the brand name could be presented in a fun and recognizable way.

02 /

Photo Referencing
(Digitalization)

During this stage, the team explored visual references to guide the digital development of the logo. The initial sketches served as the basis for refining the customized lettering and incorporating the panda-inspired details.

Different visual treatments were considered to achieve a bold, playful, and slightly edgy appearance that reflects BANDA!'s music-inspired identity. These references helped guide the translation of the hand-drawn concepts into a more refined digital design while maintaining the original idea behind the logo

03 /

Organizing
the Elements

As the concept developed, we combined bold typography with the panda-inspired details to create our custom wordmark. We wanted the logo to feel like something you would see on a band's merchandise or album cover.

04 /

BANDA! Wordmark

This helped us establish a visual identity that connects music, creativity, and the personality of BANDA!.
