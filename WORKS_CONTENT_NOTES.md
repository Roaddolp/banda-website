## Source discrepancies retained for client review

- The PDF calls pins hard enamel; the earlier development draft calls them button pins. Product titles/specifications here remain verbatim from the item list.
- Pretty Girls Love Music and Musicaholic list 25mm/32mm in the PDF, but only 25mm artwork files exist. The supplied design is shown once; no fabricated size-specific image is provided.
- Feminine Intuition, Bags Coded, and Best in Music Taste have byte-identical 25mm/32mm artwork pairs. They remain single designs, with the original combined specification.
- boys coded.png visually reads Bags coded and maps to BAGS CODED.
- Do Not Disturb describes a pink player, while the supplied illustration is predominantly purple/blue. The original description is preserved.
- Bahala Na description says neon-yellow bear face; supplied artwork is a yellow face on black. No replacement description is invented.
- The source Fake Job description includes “byOlivia” and a song attribution; text is preserved without editorial correction.
- Old numbered bracelet combination images are not separate named designs.
- Keychain back images contain embedded QR graphics. These are existing artwork, not new ordering links; their destinations have not been verified.
